## What the sources contain
- **JP before (2023-12-04):** eligible children up to the first March 31 after age 15. 15,000 yen under 3. 10,000 yen from 3 to primary school, with 15,000 for the third child onward. 10,000 yen for junior high. Special benefit of 5,000 yen above the income-limit threshold. No payment above the income cap. Payments in June, October, and February.
- **JP after (2024-07-31 and live):** eligible through the first March 31 after age 18. 15,000 yen under 3 and 10,000 yen from 3 to high school age, with 30,000 yen for the third child onward. Third-child counting includes siblings up to the first March 31 after age 22 who are financially supported. Six payments a year (even months). No income-limit text on the page.
- **US before:** Rev. Proc. 2024-40 section 2.15 gives $15,000 for unmarried individuals. The 2025 brackets for single filers are in the same document (for example 10% up to $11,925, then 12% up to $48,475).
- **US after:** the OBBBA paragraph in IRB 2025-45 states $15,750 for single individuals for tax years beginning after December 31, 2024.

These are my readings of the extracted text. They are not yet attested ground truth.

## Findings that affect the design
1. **JP replay pair.** Use the 2023-12-04 capture as "before" and the 2024-07-31 capture as "after". The 2024-07-31 page is evidence the change was knowable before the October 2024 effective period.
2. **`knownFrom` for JP.** The snapshot dates (2024-07-31) are upper bounds only. The actual enactment date is not in these sources. Find it in the law text (e-Gov) or record the snapshot date and label it as an upper bound.
3. **US `knownFrom`.** IRB 2024-45 is dated 2024-11-04, but the revenue procedure says its figures reflect law as of 2024-10-22. Choose one and record it. The July 4, 2025 enactment date is from your confirmation, not from a snapshot yet.
4. **US table quoting.** In `extracted.txt`, bracket tables break into one cell per line. A citation quote should be a single row, joined by whitespace collapsing, or the citation check will be fragile. The check will normalize whitespace.
5. **Cents.** The US brackets contain cents (`$1,192.50`), so US money must be integer cents.
6. **Page chrome.** The JP extraction includes menu and footer text. This does not matter for quote checks. The extractor does not need to be perfect.
